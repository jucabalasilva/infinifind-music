1 Find songs

Reads the Google Takeout CSV and builds a page with your songs.

2 Check availability

Checks every link in a .txt list and separates the ones that don't play in your region. The list keeps only the available ones; the others go to a file named …_unavailable.txt.

Takes about 1 second per song.
